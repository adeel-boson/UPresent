# Auth.js with email + password

We use **Auth.js** (not better-auth or a hand-rolled solution) for authentication, with **email + password plus email verification** as the sign-in method (not magic link or OAuth-only). Auth.js was picked over better-auth for its maturity and ecosystem size, even though better-auth's built-in organizations/multi-tenancy plugin maps more directly onto our `Organization`/role model — that mapping will need to be built by hand on top of Auth.js instead. Email + password was picked over magic link (avoids a hard dependency on email deliverability for every login) and OAuth-only (would exclude institutions that block personal Google logins or lack Google Workspace); email verification also doubles as the first line of defense against abuse of self-serve org signup ([ADR-0007](./0007-gated-self-serve-onboarding-sync-provisioning.md)).
